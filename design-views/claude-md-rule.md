## Design views in every planning artifact

Specs, plans, wayfinder maps/tickets, issues, PRDs and grilling records carry four views —
**Tree level · Tree stack · System design · Object** — per the **`design-views`** skill (load it
before writing one). This applies inside `superpowers:brainstorming`, `superpowers:writing-plans`,
`mattpocock-skills:wayfinder`, `wayfinder-next`, `grill-me`, `grill-with-docs` and `to-issues`
even though those skills don't mention it; it overrides their own layout where they conflict.

- **Size picks the level.** Big work (≥ 2 slices, ≥ 3 modules, > 20 files, or a shared shape
  changes) → high-level views first, wait for approval, then low-level per slice. Else low only.
- **Shorter, not longer.** One fact lives in one view; plans point at views instead of repeating
  files, fields or contracts. Grilling and review edit views in place — no new files, no changelogs.
